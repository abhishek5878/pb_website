Last month, after resigning from my data scientist job, I started digging into all the new ideas and tools people were building, debating, and deploying. Somewhere in the noise, I had a thought shouldn’t there be some order in this chaos?

That 3 am thought sparked the creation of **Mandrake Signals**.

How We Built It: Signals, Graphs, and AI.

### **Broken Engine**

The venture capital industry is experiencing unprecedented growth, with the global market projected to reach $764.78 billion by 2029 \[**[1\]](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5792967/venture-capital-investment-market-report)**. Yet beneath this explosive growth lies a fundamental contradiction: an industry powered by cutting-edge innovation that still relies on primitive discovery mechanisms.

The Convergence of VC Market Growth and AI Adoption: 2021-2029

The numbers reveal the crisis:

- **95% of early-stage companies never reach structured deal flow \[[2\]](https://www.acuitykp.com/blog/ai-in-venture-capital-transforming-start-up-evaluation/)**

- **Only 10.7% of human investor selections succeed \[[3\]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11537.pdf)**

- **82% of VC firms now use AI tools, up from 47% just one year ago \[[4\]](https://www.v7labs.com/blog/ai-for-private-equity-venture-capital)**

- **AI-managed portfolios outperform traditional approaches by 6% on average \[[5\]](https://blog.bullsmart.in/best-ai-investment-tools-portfolio-management/)**


The venture ecosystem moves billions of dollars every month, but its engine runs on human networks and guesswork. While **$126.3 billion** flowed through VC markets in Q1 2025 alone \[**[6\]](https://alterdomus.com/insight/global-venture-capital-in-2025-a-bifurcating-market/)**, most firms still operate with:

- Manual web scraping instead of automated signal processing

- Network-dependent sourcing instead of systematic market scanning

- Quarterly reporting instead of real-time intelligence streams

- Qualitative pattern recognition instead of quantitative predictive modeling


From a systems perspective, we're solving a multi-dimensional optimization problem using single-source, low-frequency data inputs. It's computationally inefficient and statistically suboptimal.

### **The $12 Billion Opportunity That's Wide Open**

I believe **venture intelligence** represents the next major SaaS category, and it's wildly underserved.

### **Immediate Addressable Market: $2 Billion**

- **15,000+ VC firms globally \[[7\]](https://dealroom.net/blog/top-venture-capital-firms)**

- **50,000+ angels and syndicates**

- **2,000+ corporate development teams** seeking competitive intelligence


These players are actively hunting for earlier access to deal flow, faster due diligence, and real-time strategic signals. But they're using tools built for analysts in 2014, not AI-native systems designed for 2025.

### **Expansion Markets: $8+ Billion**

- Growth-stage private equity firms

- Investment banks tracking private market alpha

- Hedge funds seeking alternative data sources

- Government agencies and accelerators needing ecosystem visibility

- Consulting firms conducting innovation scouting

- Universities and think tanks analyzing trend research


The core pain across all segments? **They're seeing the world after it has already changed.**

### **Engineering the Solution:**

AI-Powered Venture Intelligence System Architecture (still learning, still experimenting)

### **Signal Processing**

We ingest and analyze:

- GitHub velocity & contributor graphs

- Reddit sentiment & community growth

- Social graph patterns (who’s building, who’s funding)

- Press releases, domain registrations, job boards, partnerships


### **Knowledge Graphs**

We map relationships between

- Founders, investors, companies, markets

- Signals over time

- Competitive landscapes


### **AI-Powered Reasoning**

Ask:

> “Show me computer vision startups likely to raise Series A in Q2 2025” Mandrake replies—with confidence scores, funding timelines, valuation ranges, and supporting evidence.
