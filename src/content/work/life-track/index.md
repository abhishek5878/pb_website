<p class="thesis">An honest hole beats a flattering guess.</p>

I want to get hold of every rupee I spent, every calorie I eat, every exercise, every minute I am alive.

## One evening, ten minutes

That was the whole plan. Every evening I would narrate the day, in the same order, so nothing got skipped:

1. Money. A day with no spending still gets said out loud.
2. Food.
3. Exercise.
4. Time, in half-hour blocks. What I can't account for is logged as idle.

Then the rules. Honesty over completeness, completeness over precision. A rough entry beats a skipped one, and a skipped one beats a made-up precise one. Missed a day? Fill it in the next evening. Never let two days stack up.

For the first two to four weeks, nothing would be a bad number. Just looking. After that, budgets set from what the data actually showed.

## One afternoon

I built it on 20 August 2026, in an afternoon. Then it needed me to run a setup, and I never did. Not one day was ever logged. The bar I had set was seven days in a row.

It died at a setup screen, before the habit ever got a chance. The idea moved into [Life OS](/work/life-os/), as a paper notebook.
