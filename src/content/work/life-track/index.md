<p class="thesis">An honest hole beats a flattering guess.</p>

I want to get hold of every rupee I spent, every calorie I eat, every exercise, every minute I am alive. Numbers about my own life, instead of impressions.

## What I believed

**Honesty over completeness, completeness over precision.** A rough entry beats a skipped one. A skipped one beats a made-up precise one.

**Awareness before targets.** Two to four weeks of just looking, where nothing is a bad number yet. Then budgets set from what the data actually shows, not from guesses.

**One ritual, in a fixed order, ten minutes.** Every evening I would narrate the day and it would get written down: money, food, exercise, time. Fixed order, so nothing gets skipped.

**Zero is data.** A day with no spending still gets said out loud. Time I can't account for gets logged as idle, not smoothed over.

## What it taught me

- **It died at a setup screen, not at the habit.** I built it in one afternoon, 20 August 2026. I never ran the setup, so not one day was ever logged.
- **The bar I set and never reached:** seven days in a row, across all four.
- **The lesson moved into [Life OS](/work/life-os/),** as a paper notebook.
