<p class="thesis">Capture and ambition were never the bottleneck. Planning was.</p>

I have to get my life back on, and for that I am building systems. Not an app, and not a second brain. Those are filing cabinets; they hold your lists while they rot.

We are building this project to handle each aspect of life according to our own thinking, aspirations, and quality. This is something I want to pursue wholeheartedly.

## Three pieces

A pocket notebook, paper, with no app between me and the day. A brain that plots: plain rooms of notes, each one marked as memory or as intent. And this website, the outlet, what I choose to show.

The notebook feeds the brain. The brain feeds the site. The site is not the point; I am.

## What I believe

Planning is the part I hate. So an agent plans, and I decide. It never guilt-trips. It just re-plans.

The Google Sheets version stalled at setup; paper doesn't stall. Writing your thoughts makes them real, gives them an existence.

Nothing here is final until I've run it and it held. One aspect of life at a time.

## Where it stands

The map and the rooms exist. The notebook page and the agent don't yet. The one piece that moved is this site. My ideology is to make it exist first.
