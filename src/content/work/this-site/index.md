<p class="thesis">All of this is for me. And maybe for people to see me as well.</p>

You are here. This website will be my anchor point to not forget those things. When you paint a picture, you don't paint it for the money it will sell out for.

## What I believe

**It is made for its own sake.** No selling. Nothing on this site is for sale, and I'm not looking for anything. Genuine, real, is my forte.

**One door, the same for everyone.** An email and an ask-me-anything box. No special treatment for anybody.

**A person is shown by what he loves, not by a theme.** Point at a film or a record I love and the whole page relights in its colours. It ships lit by La La Land, which I've watched more than a hundred times.

**Show the unfinished too.** The stopped projects I still stand behind stay up, with their dates.

**Agents are readers too.** Founders, investors, friends and strangers might send their AI agents in first. We have to impress not just humans, but agents too. We should be that good. So there's an llms.txt for them.

## What it taught me

It has had five lives. The first was a template from undergrad, in 2021. In August 2026 I rebuilt it as a film site, and my note on that one was: this feels not like me. In October I bought vyas.garden and built a garden, every obsession a bed that grows. Too much metaphor, not about me. Then three more mock-ups: very AI-generated, and not like me.

What stuck is this one: a dark room with one pink light. The lesson took four tries to learn: the real thing lies in the art that I consume.

## Where it is going

I want to iterate this again and again. Still open are the spots only I can fill: poems, a mix, photos, the 2am pages. Films arrive on their own, every night, from my diary.
