<p class="thesis">All of this is for me. And maybe for people to see me as well.</p>

You are here. This website will be my anchor point to not forget those things. When you paint a picture, you don't paint it for the money it will sell out for.

## What I believe

**It is made for its own sake.** No selling. Nothing on this site is for sale, and I'm not looking for anything. Genuine, real, is my forte.

**It should be same for everyone.** No special treatment for anybody.

**The real thing lies in the art that I consume.** Point at a film or a record I love and the whole page relights in its colours. It ships lit by La La Land, which I've watched more than a hundred times.

There is a lot of projects which I stopped say midway or in the middle, and I will complete that too.

## What it taught me

It took four tries. The earlier ones were too much metaphor, and not about me.

## Where it is going

This is I am doing for myself, not for anybody else. Just I want to iterate this on again and again.
