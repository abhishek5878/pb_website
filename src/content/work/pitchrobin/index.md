My work on a digital twin for an investor, or any high-agency person. A founder opens your pitch link, pastes a deck, and goes through a simulated first call with you, in your voice and against your bar. You get back a Belief Map: what held, what was partial, what was a gap, and a meet, refer or pass. After a real call you can drop in the transcript for a debrief: what landed, and where you lost them.

The pain it was for: twenty hours a week on first-look calls, and an inbox full of decks you'll never open. Your calendar should be a moat, not a bottleneck.

It took six days in February 2026, from a triage engine on the 15th to the PitchRobin name on the 19th and the post-call debrief on the 20th. You brought your own API key; no key was stored on our servers.

The landing page is still up. The app behind it isn't, and honestly I don't know if it's still relevant.
